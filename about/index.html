About

Dr. Imen Al Samarai

I am a scientist and quantitative researcher, applying machine learning and AI where it makes a real difference. After fifteen years of research — in astroparticle physics, then in finance — I founded Pontus Analytics, which builds AI, machine-learning and physics-simulation solutions for quantitative finance, defence and R&D.

What drives me is extracting reliable signal from noisy, complex data, and turning it into systems people can use. Away from work, I am an avid reader, I compose and play music, and I make visual art.

For collaborations, roles or ventures, I am happy to talk.

The path

Education · 2008 · Paris · Marseille

Université Paris 7 Denis Diderot

MSc, Particle Physics

I love the sea, and when I heard of a neutrino detector deployed in the depths of the Mediterranean, I joined the researchers at CPPM in Marseille to work on the ANTARES experiment for my master’s thesis.

Education · 2009–2012 · Marseille

Aix-Marseille University

PhD, Particle Physics & Astrophysics

A thesis on signal detection in high-noise astrophysical data, with the ANTARES neutrino telescope deep in the Mediterranean.

  • Fibre optics
  • Silicon photon detectors
  • Particle simulation
  • High-energy physics

University of Michigan · Aix-Marseille University

Visiting Scientist · Assistant Professor

With my team, I installed the first neutrino alert system to trigger a network of robotic telescopes around the globe. I also taught statistics, physics and mathematics to undergraduate and graduate students.

  • Optical astronomy

Physics · 2013–2016 · Paris · Mendoza

LPNHE, Paris

Postdoctoral Researcher

The astrophysical neutrinos I searched for during my thesis are closely tied to the highest-energy cosmic rays. Following that thread, I joined the Pierre Auger Observatory, spread across the Pampa Amarilla in Mendoza, Argentina, to detect them. I also explored the radio detection of cosmic rays: I developed the first theoretical model of the emission, deployed radio antennas in the pampa with my own hands, and analysed the data they collected.

  • Phenomenology
  • Theoretical physics
  • Radio detection
  • Signal processing
  • GHz signals
Physics · 2016–2018 · Geneva

CERN

Postdoctoral Researcher

Back to neutrinos, on a far larger detector than ANTARES: IceCube, buried in the ice at the South Pole. After the 2017 neutrino alert, I was among the researchers who went back through almost a decade of data and found a burst of 13 neutrinos from the blazar TXS 0506+056 in 2014–15, published in Science in 2018. In parallel, I worked on the Cherenkov Telescope Array, as first author of the calibration and performance studies of the SiPM camera for the SST-1M telescope prototype. I also supervised three PhD researchers.

  • Machine learning
  • Random forests
  • Neutrino source searches
  • Multi-messenger astronomy
  • Big-data analysis
  • SiPM cameras
  • Detector calibration
  • PhD supervision
Markets · 2018–2020 · Paris

Swiss Life

Lead Data Scientist & ML Engineer

At CERN I had joined the finance club, where physicists discussed how their methods carry over to financial markets. It caught my curiosity, and I moved to finance to explore markets first-hand. At Swiss Life I designed and deployed a production robo-advisory engine combining LSTM networks and genetic algorithms, serving thousands of clients, and built the full machine-learning pipeline behind it: client profiling, automated rebalancing and real-time risk monitoring. I led a team of three data scientists.

  • Production deployment
  • Financial data APIs
  • Data analysis
  • Databases
  • Docker & Kubernetes
  • LSTM networks
  • Genetic algorithms
Markets · 2020–2024 · London

TD Securities

Director, Quantitative Research

As Director of Quantitative Research, I built TD’s systematic fixed-income research platform from concept to firm-wide adoption across trading desks, with machine-learning alpha signals, validated backtesting frameworks and production NLP tools for central-bank sentiment. I grew the team from a single contributor to seven PhD-level researchers and expanded the research to multi-asset strategies in FX and commodities. The work earned the Above and Beyond Award (2021) and the Legendary Award (2023) for systematic research leadership.

  • Team leadership
  • Macro
  • Fixed income
  • Systematic trading
  • Bloomberg & Reuters data
  • NLP
  • Databases
AI · Now · Paris

Pontus Analytics

Founder & CEO

Pontus Analytics provides scientific and AI advisory to teams building advanced products. We support complex projects that need physics or machine-learning expertise, from the first idea to deployment: Monte Carlo simulation, state-space models, reinforcement-learning environments and evaluations. Alongside, I research how language models plan under uncertainty.

  • Scientific advisory
  • Monte Carlo simulation
  • State-space models
  • Reinforcement learning
  • AI evaluation
